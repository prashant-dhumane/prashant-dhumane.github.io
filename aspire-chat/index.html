Questions ✓✓

Before you buy

Do I need Firebase, Pusher or any paid service?

No. Everything runs on your own server: the database, live updates, photos and files.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay once. Updates for the first year are included.

Can I use it for client projects?

Yes, on as many of your own and your clients' sites as you like. You may not resell or share the code itself.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, in the phone's browser, with a layout made for small screens. There is no separate app, so there are no phone push notifications. For people who are away, your site can send an SMS, WhatsApp or email when they get a message.

Does it use my existing users and logins?

Yes. Your site stays in charge of who is logged in. It gives the chat a short-lived key for that user, so there are no separate chat accounts or passwords.

Can one chat server serve several sites?

Yes. Each site gets its own key, and users of one site never see another site's users or messages.

Can I change the code or the look?

Yes. You get the full source. Colours, labels and language can be set without touching the code.

Group chats or video calls?

Not in this version. Aspire Chat is one-to-one chat. Group chat, voice messages and calls are planned for a later version.
